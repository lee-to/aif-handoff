# Keep queued tasks out of stale execution recovery

**Date:** 2026-11-05 18:30 MSK
**Files:** packages/data/src/index.ts, packages/data/src/taskTransitions.ts, packages/data/src/taskOwnership.ts, packages/agent/src/coordinator.ts, packages/agent/src/taskWatchdog.ts, packages/agent/src/__tests__/attemptRecoveryRollout.test.ts, recovery documentation and checklist
**Severity:** high

## Problem

Issue #164: an unclaimed review waiting behind a busy stage was moved to `blocked_external` and charged a retry after the stale timeout.

## Root Cause

Stage-start recording and watchdog eligibility were coupled to the optional attempt-recovery rollout, disabled by default. The default watchdog classified queue age as execution liveness.

## Solution

Always record the existing `stageStartedAt` marker and fresh liveness timestamps on coordinator claims. Preserve it when the coordinator starts the claimed stage, clear it on stage exits, explicit task actions and ownership handoffs, and require it for watchdog scans. Keep attempt fencing and retry-debt policy behind their existing flag. Preserve operational recovery logs and add a debug candidate count without temporary ticket prefixes. No migration is needed; legacy rows acquire a marker on their next actual claim.

Regression-first verification: the two default-mode queue scenarios failed before implementation (`review` became `blocked_external` and spent retry debt), while their enabled-mode equivalents passed. The unchanged regression check passed after the fix; the expanded rollout suite has eight passing cases. All 3,269 unit/integration tests pass. Every package meets all 70% coverage thresholds; format, lint, build, protocol sync, five browser checks and three k6 scripts pass. The aggregate `npm run ai:validate` required separate reruns after parallel test timeouts and sandbox socket restrictions; tests were rerun with bounded workers and real-socket suites outside the sandbox. Performance checks used a dedicated temporary SQLite database.

## Prevention

- Test queue exclusion and real interrupted-execution recovery with the rollout flag both off and on.
- Make queued fixtures unclaimed and hung fixtures actually claimed before advancing the clock.
- Keep stage-start lifecycle independent of optional fencing and retry-budget behavior.
- Verify the coordinator preserves claim evidence across `plan_ready` to `implementing` transitions; consider an end-to-end saturated-capacity scenario for broader scheduler coverage.
